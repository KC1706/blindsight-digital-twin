# Speaker notes & timing (#55)

Voice: factual, unhurried, let the facts land. Total ≈ 10 min (5–6 slides + 4 min demo).
Practice the transitions — the deck's only job is to set up the live demo.

---

**1 · Title (10s).** "Blindsight — a digital twin that sees the stations it can't measure.
The stations that most need watching are usually the ones nothing can see."

**2 · The blind line (35s).** Tell the 9:14 story as a fact, not a pitch: *"At 9:14 a torque
tool drifts 4% out of spec. No alarm. The line keeps moving. It's found at 2pm at end-of-line
audit — by then hundreds of cars carry it."* Then: ~40 stations, ~35% dark, and the dark ones
are exactly the manual stations where the bottleneck hides.

**3 · The insight (40s).** The turn. *"You don't need new sensors. The line already knows."*
Blocked/starved points at the constraint; travel times between scans are equations you solve
for every station. Land the phrase: **the line is the sensor.**

**3b · Hero visual (20s, optional).** Let the animation run a few seconds while you say the
dark stations light up with *estimates*, not measurements.

**4 · How it works (35s).** Walk the five moves left to right, one breath each. End on "three
views, one model" — that's the demo structure.

**5 · See the invisible (25s).** "3.3% error at stations with no sensor — and 100% of our
error bars actually contain the truth. We're accurate *and* honest about it."

**6 · Forecast (30s).** takt_slip_s14. "No sensor on S14, yet we finger it and forecast ~15
units at risk over two hours — 90 minutes before a rear-view dashboard would."

**7 · Live & recursive (25s).** "This isn't a hindsight report. The filter updates every tick
and catches a regime change in about five minutes." (Merged this round — mention it's new.)

**8 · Mixed-model (25s).** "Real lines are mixed-model. We recover per-variant work content —
a sunroof on one trim — and localize it to the exact dark station. And we're honest about what
a no-overtaking line can't reveal."

**9 · Trust (25s).** "Zero false alarms across 12 clean shifts — that's the number that decides
whether a crew ever trusts a system. Forecast ~10× better than persistence."

**10 · Business case (30s).** "Retrofit-free — software on data you already emit. ~$7.5M/yr on
one line, base case. Sub-month payback, and read that honestly: it's because line-blindness
loss is large, not because the tool is expensive."

**11 · Roadmap + ask (30s).** "It even tells you where to spend: instrument S14 first, it
removes the most uncertainty. The ask: one line, read-only, one pilot shift."

**12 · Demo (4:00).** Switch to the live dashboard. Follow the six beats. If anything stalls,
cut to the fallback recording without comment and keep narrating.

**Close (15s).** "No retrofit. No new sensors. Just the line, finally telling you what it
already knows."

## If you're short on time
Drop 3b (hero), 8 (mixed-model), and shorten 4. Never drop 9 (trust) or the demo.
